## How to add yourself

1. In the same pull request as your first contribution, add one entry under
   **Contributors**, in the order people joined (newest at the bottom).
2. Use this format: your name in bold, your GitHub handle as a link if you want one,
   then one line on what you worked on, written as plain nouns and verbs:

   ```markdown
   * **Your Name** ([@handle](https://github.com/handle)): what you worked on.
   ```

3. Do not add email addresses or other personal contact details.
4. If you add a font, icon set, library or other third-party asset, credit it under
   **Third-party work** with its licence, and put the licence text in `licenses/`
   when the licence requires it.

Keep your entry up to date when you make a larger contribution later. The commit
history stays the full record of who changed what; this file is the readable summary.
